🌲 About GreenPath

GreenPath is Google Maps for Sustainable Travel — integrating multi-modal route finding with real-time carbon intelligence, transit detection, and personal carbon offset tracking.

🎯 Our Mission

Empower travelers to reduce urban greenhouse emissions by comparing travel time, cost, distance, and CO₂ output across all practical transport modes.

⚙️ Technology Stack

Built with Django, PostgreSQL, OpenStreetMap Nominatim, OSRM Routing Engine, Leaflet.js, and Web Speech Navigation Synthesis.
